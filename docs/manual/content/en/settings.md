**Settings** is in the user menu. What you choose there is saved with your account, so it follows you
to every device.

- **Language** — of the screens, and of the mail meant for you.
- **Theme** — System, Light or Dark.
- **Time zone** — for every date and time shown.
- **Invitation mail** — on by default. Off, invitations still wait under **Invitations**; they are only
  not mailed.
- **Weekly summary** — off by default (see *Dashboard and activity*).
- **Picture** — upload a JPEG, PNG or GIF of up to 5 MB. It is cropped to a square and stored without
  its metadata, such as where a photo was taken. Without one, you are shown by your initials, or by the
  picture your identity provider supplies.

## Your account {#account}

Your name and email come from your identity provider, which updates them at each sign-in. With an
email and password account you can change your name here, and **Change password**; changing it signs
out your other sessions.

**Delete account** removes your account. Employers you own alone are deleted with it — the page names
them first — and employers you share are simply left. You confirm by typing your email address. The
only administrator of an installation cannot delete their account.
