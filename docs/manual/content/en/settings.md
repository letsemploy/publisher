**Settings** is in the user menu. What you choose there is saved with your account, so it follows you
to every device. It has a section for each kind of setting, listed on its left, and each saves only
what is on it:

- **Profile** — your **picture**, name and email. Choose a JPEG, PNG or GIF of up to 5 MB and it is
  uploaded at once. It is cropped to a square and stored without its metadata, such as where a photo
  was taken. Without one, you are shown by your initials, or by the picture your identity provider
  supplies.
- **Display** — the **language** of the screens and of the mail meant for you, the **theme** (System,
  Light or Dark) and the **time zone** for every date and time shown.
- **Notifications** — **invitation mail**, on by default; off, invitations still wait under
  **Invitations**, they are only not mailed. The **weekly summary**, off by default (see *Dashboard
  and activity*).

## Your account {#account}

Your name and email come from your identity provider, which updates them at each sign-in. With an
email and password account you can change your name on **Profile**, and your password under
**Password**; changing it signs out your other sessions.

**Delete account**, set apart at the foot of the list, removes your account. Employers you own alone are deleted with it — the page names
them first — and employers you share are simply left. You confirm by typing your email address. The
only administrator of an installation cannot delete their account.
