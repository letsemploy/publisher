## Dashboard {#dashboard}

The **Dashboard** covers the employer you are working on:

- **status counts** of published, draft, expired and inactive jobs, each linking to the filtered list;
- **job link clicks** over the last 30 days, compared with the 30 days before, per day and by country,
  and the most clicked jobs;
- **needs attention**: published jobs closing within 14 days, active jobs that cannot be published, and
  drafts nobody has touched for 30 days;
- the latest **activity**, your **feeds** and your **permalinks** with their URLs.

## Activity {#activity}

**Activity** is the employer's log: who did what, and when, newest first. Owners see all of it; editors
see everything except invitations and API tokens, which are owners' business. **My activity** in the
user menu is your own log: what you did, and what was done to you, such as being invited or suspended.

## Weekly summary {#summary}

Turn on the weekly summary in **Settings** to get a mail each week about the employers you belong to:
the counts, clicks, newly published and deactivated jobs, jobs whose date window closed, what needs
attention, and, for owners, tokens about to expire. A quiet week sends nothing.
