## Signing in {#sign-in}

The sign-in page offers whatever your installation has set up: a button for each identity provider
(for example your company's single sign-on, Google or GitHub), and, where it is enabled, an email and
password form with links to **sign up** and to reset a forgotten password. A provider's button takes
you to that provider; you never enter its password here.

Signing up with an email address sends you a link. You choose your password on the page that link
opens, which also confirms the address.

## Your first employer {#first-employer}

Everything you work on belongs to an **employer**: its jobs, feeds, locations, tags and people. You
get access to one in either of two ways:

- **Create one** with **New employer**. You become its owner, and it starts with its headquarters and a
  feed called `all`.
- **Accept an invitation.** Someone who owns an employer invites you by your email address; the
  invitation appears under **Invitations** in the top bar, naming the role you are offered.
  **Accept** takes you straight to that employer's dashboard; **Decline** only removes the invitation.

## Finding your way around {#navigation}

- **The sidebar** is about the employer you are working on. It starts with the **employer switcher**:
  if you belong to several employers, choose the one to work on there. Below it are **Dashboard**,
  **Jobs**, **Feeds**, **People**, **API tokens** (owners only), **Profile**, **Locations**, **Tags** and
  **Activity**, all covering that employer.
- **The top bar** holds what is not about one employer: **Employers**, **Invitations** with the number
  still waiting, and your user menu with **My activity**, **Settings**, **Welcome** (the short introduction you saw after your first sign-in), this manual and signing out.

## Roles {#roles}

Each person has a role per employer:

| Role | May |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | everything: the employer record, people and invitations, API tokens, deleting the employer |
| **Editor** | work on jobs, feeds, permalinks, locations and tags |

Every member sees who else belongs to the employer. An employer always keeps at least one active
owner.
