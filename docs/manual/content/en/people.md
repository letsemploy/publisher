## The People page {#people}

**People** lists everyone who belongs to the employer, with their role. Every member sees it; owners
also get the controls below. If you are an editor and need access changed, the list tells you whom to
ask.

## Inviting someone {#invite}

Owners invite with an email address and a role, **Editor** by default. The person must already have an
account here — ask them to sign in once first. They find the invitation under **Invitations** and
receive a mail about it unless they turned that off in their settings. Nobody gets access without
accepting.

The form answers the same whether or not the address belongs to an account, so it cannot be used to
find out who is registered. It does tell you when the person is already a member or already invited.
Pending invitations are listed with a **Revoke** action.

## Roles, removal and suspension {#roles}

On each member an owner can:

- **change the role** between owner and editor;
- **remove** the member, who may be invited again later;
- **suspend** the member: they lose access at once, but keep their row and role and get them back with
  **Reinstate**. You cannot suspend yourself.

The last active owner cannot be demoted, removed or suspended: make someone else owner first. The page
says so where the control would be.

## Deleting the employer {#delete-employer}

Owners can delete the employer from its **Profile**. The confirmation lists what goes with it — jobs,
feeds, members, API tokens, locations, tags and invitations — and asks you to type the employer's
name. Its public feed URLs answer *not found* from then on.
