## Creating a job {#create}

**New job** opens the form, grouped as *Basics* (title, description, URL, language, reference ID,
category), *Classification* (type, mode, level, tags), *Workload and salary*, *Dates* and
*Locations*. The URL is where applicants should go — your careers page or your
applicant tracking system.

A new job is saved as a **draft**. **Save draft** keeps it that way; **Save and activate** also tries to
make it active, and if something is missing it saves the draft and lists exactly what.

## States {#states}

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Being prepared. Incomplete data is fine. Never published. |
| **Active** | Meant to be published. |
| **Inactive** | Withdrawn, filled or archived. Kept for the record, never published. |

A draft is activated, or deleted if it is not wanted; an active job can go back to draft or be
deactivated; an inactive one can be activated again. The date a job was first activated is kept as
its publication date and does not change when you edit it later.

## Publication readiness {#readiness}

A job can only be activated when it meets every publication requirement. Its page shows them as a
checklist, **Publication readiness**, with each unmet one linked to the field that fixes it:

- the status is active and the publication date is set;
- title, URL, language and type are present;
- at least one location;
- if a salary is given, its currency and interval too;
- it is within its date window.

## The date window {#date-window}

A job leaves its feeds by itself when its **apply before** date or its **end date** has passed — nobody has
to edit it. It stays active, and comes back by itself if you move the date. The **start date** never
removes a job: a position starting in three months is a normal posting.

## What a job shows as {#shown-as}

The list and the job's page show what the job currently presents as:

| Shown as | Why |
|---|---|
| **Published** | Active, complete and within its date window: it is in its feeds. |
| **Expired** | Active, but its apply before or end date has passed. |
| **Incomplete** | Active, but a requirement is not met any more, for example its last location was removed. |
| **Draft** | A draft. |
| **Inactive** | Deactivated. |

Only **published** jobs appear in a feed. The job's page also lists its feeds and its **status history**: who
changed its status, from what to what, and when.

## Job links and clicks {#clicks}

In the published feed, a job's link points to this application, which counts the click and forwards
the visitor to your URL. Clicks are counted per day and country only — no address and no browser
details are stored. The dashboard shows them. Once a job is no longer published, its link tells the
visitor that the posting is gone instead of forwarding.

## Deleting a job {#delete}

Possible in any state. The confirmation names the feeds the job will disappear from.
