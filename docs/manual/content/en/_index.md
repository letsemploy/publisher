---
title: "User manual"
---

oJobPub Publisher is where an employer's team keeps its job postings and publishes the ones it
chooses as a machine-readable **`ojobpub.json`** feed. Job boards, aggregators, partner sites and
your own website read that feed, instead of copying postings by hand or scraping your pages.

It is deliberately small. It is **not** a job board: job seekers never come here, they find your
postings through the sites reading your feed. It is **not** an applicant tracking system: every
posting links to wherever you actually take applications. And it is **not** a CMS: a posting carries
a short plain description, not a designed page.

The usual way through it:

1. Sign in and create your employer, or accept an invitation to an existing one.
2. Create your jobs and activate them once they are complete.
3. Choose which jobs a feed publishes. Every employer starts with a feed holding all of them.
4. Create a **permalink** and give its URL to whoever reads your postings.
