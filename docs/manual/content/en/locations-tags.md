## Profile {#profile}

**Profile** is the employer record: name, slug, website, industry and headquarters. Owners edit it;
editors see it. The headquarters is one of the employer's locations, or a new city and country that
then joins them.

## Locations {#locations}

**Locations** are the places your jobs are at; a job needs at least one to be published. They belong to
the employer and are offered in the job form's location picker. A location still used by the
headquarters or by a job cannot be deleted — the page names what uses it.

## Tags {#tags}

**Tags** label jobs, for example by team or skill, and are published with them. Names are lower-case,
at most 28 characters, and unique within the employer. The list shows how many jobs carry each tag;
deleting one says how many jobs will lose it. Renaming a tag changes it on every job at once.
